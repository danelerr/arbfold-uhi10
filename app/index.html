The mental model

Why are there three pools?

A cyclic arbitrage exists when three exchange rates disagree. A trader can move through the triangle and finish with more of the asset they started with.

Why it matters

Arbitrage is useful.
Replaying every step may not be.

Arbitrage brings AMM prices back into alignment. ARBFOLD does not try to stop it. It asks a systems question: when cooperating pools can verify the destination, can Uniswap v4 reach it without executing every intermediate trade?

02

Same trader result

03

v4-native accounting

PoolManager claims let cooperating hooks move backed reserves atomically.

Important boundary

ARBFOLD is a specialized experiment for new hook-owned pools. It does not attach to existing Uniswap liquidity and it does not claim material LP net-value improvement.
