The intuition

Why execute every intermediate trade if the destination is already verifiable?

Arbitrage normally reconciles these pools through three additional swaps. ARBFOLD asks whether cooperating pools need to replay those swaps when they can verify and apply the equivalent final state directly.

Research boundary

Built for new hook-owned pools. It cannot attach to existing Uniswap pools and does not claim material LP net-value improvement.
